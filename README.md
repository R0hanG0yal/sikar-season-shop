##  Key Features

### 1. Modern Front-End E-Commerce Storefront (Flatlogic / 21st.dev Style)
- **Pure White Background Images**: Custom-rendered studio vector product illustrations engineered on pure `#FFFFFF` background with subtle contact shadows so they blend seamlessly into product cards with zero bounding boxes.
- **Header & Navigation**: Royal brand monogram, dynamic category mega-bar, live search bar with autocomplete, wishlist drawer, and cart slide-over.
- **Hero Showcase Carousel**: Seasonal Rajasthani festive banners, promotional tags, and direct one-click switch to the POS Billing Terminal.
- **Product Card Experience**: Real-time stock counters, discount badges, quick view popup with pincode delivery estimator (Sikar same-day delivery), and wishlist hearts.
- **Cart & Interactive Checkout**: Coupon codes (`SIKAR10`, `POOJA20`, `WELCOME50`), instant UPI QR code generation, Doorstep Delivery vs. Sikar Store Pickup, and celebration confetti.

### 2. Mall-Grade Franchise Billing Software & POS (Like DMart / Vishal Mega Mart)
- **High-Speed Barcode Gun Scanner**: Real-time barcode/SKU input with auto-focus and auditory scan "beep" feedback.
- **Hold & Resume Bills (DMart Cashier Feature)**: Cashiers can place a customer's bill on hold with one click, serve subsequent customers, and recall the bill instantly.
- **Customer CRM & Loyalty Points**: Auto-detects customer by 10-digit mobile number, tracks lifetime spend, and allows redeeming loyalty points for cash discounts.
- **Multi-Mode Tender Settlement**:
  - **Cash**: Enter Cash Received to automatically calculate Change Returned to customer.
  - **Dynamic UPI QR Code**: Instant scan-and-pay QR code generated with exact bill amount.
  - **Card POS**: Records card auth reference.
  - **Khata / Store Credit**: Adds to customer credit ledger with pending balance tracking.
- **Authentic 80mm Thermal Receipt & GST Tax Invoice**: Standard supermarket receipt format featuring shop details, cashier ID, itemized HSN codes, tax summary, big savings highlight (" YOU SAVED ₹XXX TODAY!"), and scannable barcode.
- **Inventory & Live Stock Ledger**: Tracks stock units, flags low-stock warnings (<15 units), and enables bulk stock-in adjustments (+5, +20).
- **Physical Barcode Price Tag Generator**: Prints sheets of barcode stickers with shop name, SKU, MRP, our price, and scannable barcodes to stick directly on retail products.
- **Daily Sales Z-Report (Day End Reconciliation)**: Tally of total revenue, cash drawer reconciliation, UPI digital intake, and CGST/SGST tax liabilities.
